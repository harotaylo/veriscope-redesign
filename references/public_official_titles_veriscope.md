## NOTES FOR IMPLEMENTATION

- **Categorization**: Group by jurisdiction level (Federal, State, County, Local) and function (Elected, Appointed, Law Enforcement, Judicial)
- **Aliases**: Many titles have variations (e.g., "Congressman" vs "U.S. Representative", "Police Officer" vs "Cop")
- **Hierarchy**: Consider tracking rank/level within law enforcement (Officer → Sergeant → Lieutenant → Captain → Chief)
- **Jurisdiction**: Pair with jurisdiction_id (state/federal district/county/municipality)
- **Case Matching**: Filter for specific misconduct patterns by title (e.g., excessive force for police, campaign finance for elected)
